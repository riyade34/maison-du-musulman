Our story

A universe born from a simple need

It all began with a frustration many of us know: looking for a prayer rug that doesn’t lose its shape after three months, a truly pure nigella oil, a well-cut qamis — and finding, shop after shop, poorly made products or unreliable websites.

I told myself there had to be a better way: a shop designed with the same standards you would apply to your own home. Not an endless catalogue, but a carefully limited selection — things you would genuinely choose, because they have a real place in the daily life of a Muslim.

L’Univers du Croyant was born from this idea: bringing together in one place everything that accompanies prayer, clothing, wellbeing and the home — with care, without excess, and with the sincere hope that every order will serve you well for a long time.

Riyade, founder
