Returns and refunds

The steps to follow to exercise your right of withdrawal or to report a non-conforming product.

This English translation is provided for information only. The French version prevails in case of any discrepancy.

Last updated: 16 September 2026

1. Withdrawal within 14 days

Except where the law provides otherwise, you have 14 days from receipt of the product to notify your decision to withdraw, without giving a reason. Use the “Withdraw from contract here” feature: an acknowledgement of receipt must be sent to you by email.

2. Returning the product

After notification, send the product back within the legal time limit to: [RETURN ADDRESS TO BE COMPLETED]. The product may have been handled only to the extent necessary to check its nature, characteristics and proper functioning. A reduction in value resulting from excessive handling may be taken into account under the legal conditions.

Return costs: [STATE WHETHER RETURN COSTS ARE BORNE BY THE CUSTOMER OR THE SELLER]. This rule must be settled and published before any sale.

3. Refund

The refund includes the sums legally due, including standard delivery costs where the law so provides. It is made within the legal time limit using the same means of payment, unless expressly agreed otherwise. The seller may defer the refund until the product is received or proof of dispatch is provided.

4. Damaged or non-conforming product

If the product is damaged, incomplete or non-conforming, contact the seller promptly via the contact page, stating the order reference and, if useful, photographs. The legal guarantees apply independently of the right of withdrawal.

5. Exceptions

The right of withdrawal may be excluded in the cases provided for by law, in particular for certain goods made to the consumer’s specifications or clearly personalised, or unsealed goods that cannot be returned for reasons of hygiene or health protection. Any applicable exception must be clearly stated on the product page before purchase.
